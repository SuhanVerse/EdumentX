# BasoBas — Product Vision
## Who We Are, Who We Serve, Why We Exist

---

## Vision Statement

BasoBas exists to eliminate the broker from the Nepali rental
process entirely. We replace the middleman with a structured,
verified, and privacy-preserving platform where tenants and
landlords connect directly — saving tenants tens of thousands
of rupees and saving landlords from privacy risks and
unqualified callers.

---

## The Traditional Nepal Rental Journey

```
Step 1:  Tenant hears about a room through word of mouth
Step 2:  Tenant calls a broker (dalal)
Step 3:  Broker arranges visits to 5–10 random rooms
Step 4:  Most rooms are wrong type, wrong price, wrong area
Step 5:  Tenant wastes 2–6 weeks visiting wrong properties
Step 6:  Tenant finally finds a room
Step 7:  Broker charges a lot of commission
Step 8:  Landlord sometimes pays broker separately too

Total broker cost to tenant:  MINIMUM NPR 5000 
Total time wasted:            2 – 6 weeks
Wrong properties visited:     5 – 15
```

---

## The BasoBas Journey

```
Step 1:  Tenant opens BasoBas
Step 2:  Tenant browses verified listings with real photos
Step 3:  AI recommendations surface the best matches
Step 4:  Tenant requests a visit with preferred date and time
Step 5:  Landlord approves — exact address unlocked
Step 6:  Tenant visits, moves in
Step 7:  Zero broker commission. Zero middleman.

Cost:                         NPR 0 (free) or NPR 249/mo (Pro)
Time to find:                 Days, not weeks
Wrong properties visited:     Minimal — AI + filters help
Broker cost saved:            NPR 15,000 – 30,000
```

## Who Uses BasoBas

### The Tenant

Someone actively searching for a rental room, apartment, or house.

**Primary need:** Find a verified room quickly, directly, without paying a broker.

```
Typical profile:
  Age:         22 – 35
  Occupation:  Young professional, student, fresh graduate
  Budget:      NPR 5,000 – 25,000 per month
  Location:    Moving to or within Kathmandu Valley
  Pain point:  Wasted time and money on brokers and wrong visits
```

**What tenants can do:**
- Browse all rental listings freely
- View property details, photos, and facilities
- See approximate location (never exact until approved)
- Submit visit requests with preferred date and time
- Receive approval, reschedule, or rejection from landlord
- Access exact location only after visit is approved
- Follow up post-visit with their interest level
- Rate and review landlords after visit completion
- Save favourite properties
- Upgrade to Pro plan for enhanced features

---

### The Landlord

A property owner with one or more rooms or units available for rent.

**Primary need:** Find a reliable, verified tenant without broker fees or privacy risks.

```
Typical profile:
  Age:        35 – 60
  Situation:  Owns extra room, floor, or property in Kathmandu
  Pain point: Shares address with random callers who never show up
  Concern:    Wants to screen tenants before revealing location
```

**What landlords can do:**
- Create structured property listings
- Control when and to whom exact address is revealed
- Accept, reschedule, or reject visit requests
- Manage multiple applicants per property
- Mark a rental as finalized
- Update property to Occupied status
- View tenant profile and rating before accepting
- Get identity verified for tenant trust

---

### Dual Role Users

A single account can hold both Tenant and Landlord roles simultaneously.

**Common scenario:** Someone renting a room while also subletting
a room in their own property.

Switching between roles is instant from the Profile screen.
The home screen, navigation dock, and feed adapt to whichever
role is currently active. No re-login required.

## Core Principles

### 1. Privacy First — The Address Rule

The exact property address and precise GPS pin are **never
revealed to any tenant** unless that specific tenant has an
active visit request with status ACCEPTED.

```
What ALL users see publicly:
  Locality/area name:        "Pulchowk, Lalitpur"
  Approximate blurred map

What ONLY the approved tenant sees:
  Full street address:       "123 Naya Bato, Near Engineering College"
  Exact GPS pin on map
  Navigation access
  Landlord phone (optional — at landlord's discretion)
```

This rule is non-negotiable and cannot be bypassed.

---

### 2. No Broker, No Commission

BasoBas charges zero commission on any rental transaction.
The platform connects parties directly. The financial
relationship between tenant and landlord exists entirely
outside the app.

---

### 3. Verification Builds Trust

Landlords must verify their identity (Citizenship or NID)
before any listing can go live. This is mandatory and
cannot be bypassed for any reason.

Tenants can optionally verify identity. Verified tenants
receive a trust badge visible to landlords on all requests.

---

### 4. Phone Number Is Identity

The only authentication method is phone number OTP.

```
No email login
No Google Sign-In
No Apple Sign-In
No password

A Nepal (+977) phone number is the user's permanent identity.
```

---

### 5. Broker Replacement, Not Broker Supplement

BasoBas is not a tool to help brokers work more efficiently.
It is a direct replacement for the broker entirely.
Every feature is designed to make the broker unnecessary.
